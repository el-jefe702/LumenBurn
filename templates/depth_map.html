Post-Processing Pipeline

  • Upscaling: It loads the standard 8-bit image (0-255 grayscale) generated by the AI and mathematically upscales it into a high-precision 16-bit space (0-65535). This gives the image the "headroom" needed for smooth math without introducing pixelation.
  • Inpainting (Hole Fixing): It looks for pure black pixels (missing data) and intelligently fills them in based on the surrounding pixels so that sudden drops don't cause the laser to drill a hole in your material.
  • Bilateral & Gaussian Filtering: It applies a heavy bilateral filter. This is the magic step that removes the "stair-stepping" or banding artifacts common in AI depth maps while keeping the sharp edges of the actual subject intact. It then adds a slight Gaussian blur to smooth out micro-textures.
  • Normalization: It stretches the highest peaks to pure white and the lowest valleys to pure black, ensuring you get the maximum possible contrast and physical depth from your material when laser engraving.
  • Lossless Export: Finally, it saves the result as a heavily compressed, lossless 16-bit PNG (forge_...png) ready to be imported into LightBurn.
